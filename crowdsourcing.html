Crowdsourcing ideas with visitors

This page is for collecting ideas, reactions, and small contributions from potential visitors so we can shape the digital MTA around how people actually want to explore it.

01

vote on what matters

Ask visitors to pick which views, filters, or story paths they would use first.

  • Choose the most useful object grouping
  • Rank the clearest labels or tags
  • Mark the view you would click first
02

tag patterns and themes

Invite people to add words that describe what the dot-based views should highlight.

  • Object type
  • Location or origin
  • Time period, style, or story
03

submit a contribution

Let visitors share one idea, one question, or one example of a museum object they would want to find.

  • A favorite object topic
  • A search question
  • A missing feature or idea

Five questions for participants

Use these prompts to gather fast feedback from a crowd, a workshop group, or a visitor test session.

1

What would make you want to contribute here?

Finds the motivation that could drive participation.

2

What kind of museum object would you submit first?

Reveals which object types feel most relevant.

3

What would you expect to happen after you add a tag or vote?

Checks whether the feedback loop feels clear.

4

What would make this feel trustworthy and worth your time?

Surfaces trust, value, and moderation needs.

5

What would make the crowdsourcing flow too long or confusing?

Identifies friction before the page is finalized.
